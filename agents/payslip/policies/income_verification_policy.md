# Income Verification Policy

## 1. Declared Income

The declared monthly income must be supported by the submitted payslip.

## 2. Income Consistency

The declared monthly income should match the verified income shown on the payslip.

## 3. Income Overstatement

If the declared monthly income is higher than the income supported by the payslip, the application should be rejected or flagged for manual review.

## 4. Salary Calculation

The system should verify that:

Gross Salary = Basic Salary + Allowances

Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions

If the salary components are mathematically inconsistent, the payslip should be flagged.

## 5. Evidence

The verification decision should record the policy document and section used as evidence.
